---

A tModLoader experiment retaining in-game perception, navigation, action execution,
and decision-model-driven boss combat.

Jev requests go through Decision Infra; provider credentials and model weights stay
outside the mod. Local HTTP is limited to five observability endpoints: /health,
/state, /jev, /jev_log, and /jev_clear.

Automatic pot smashing and cobweb clearing remain internal navigation behavior.

Source and details: https://github.com/hufaei/TerraBlind
